Business and Technical views

Two ways to see the same requests, depending on your role.

Youm shows the same requests in two ways. The Business / Technical switch at the top of the screen moves between them: the data stays the same, only the presentation changes.

Business view: my requests

The Business view is for the people who express needs and validate what is delivered. It shows each request’s status in business words, who it is with and the next step, without points or sprints.

Every request follows the same path: Request sent, Accepted, Planned, In progress, Awaiting validation, Validated.

Technical view: the backlog

The Technical view is for the Scrum team. The Proposals, Backlog, Validated and All tabs sort stories by progress; the list shows their type, status and author. The Backlog tab is described in the chapter “Estimate and rank the backlog”.

Decide on a proposal

The Product Owner opens a proposal from the Proposals tab and chooses:

  • Accept: it enters the backlog;
  • Decline: they enter a reason, required, which the author will read;
  • Reopen, for a declined proposal: it becomes Suggested again.

Only the Product Owner takes these decisions. For others, the buttons are greyed out and the reason is written below.

In this version, the Filter and Sort buttons, as well as search, are visible but not active yet.
