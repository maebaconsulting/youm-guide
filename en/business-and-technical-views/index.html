Business and Technical views

Two ways to see the same requests, depending on your role.

Youm shows the same requests in two ways. The Business / Technical switch at the top of the screen moves between them: the data stays the same, only the presentation changes.

Business view: my requests

The Business view is for the people who express needs and validate what is delivered. It shows each request’s status in business words and the next step, without points or sprints. Each status has its colour, from the grey of a sent request to the green of a validated one.

Every request follows the same path: Request sent, Accepted, Planned, In progress, Awaiting validation, Validated.

Technical view: the backlog

The Technical view is for the Scrum team. A side menu, on the left, lists the Projects then the Views of the current project, each with its number of items: Proposals, Backlog, Planning, Task Board, Validated, All and Features. On a phone, the Menu button opens it as a drawer; it closes after a choice, with Escape or with a tap outside. The list shows each story’s type, status and author, with type and status in colour. The Backlog view is described in the chapter “Estimate and rank the backlog”.

Team notes

In the Technical view, a story’s details include Team notes: implementation details, in Markdown, that the team and the Product Owner write with Edit. They never show in the Business view.

Decide on a proposal

The Product Owner opens a proposal from the Proposals view and chooses:

  • Accept: it enters the backlog;
  • Decline: they enter a reason, required, which the author will read;
  • Reopen, for a declined proposal: it becomes Suggested again.

Only the Product Owner takes these decisions. For others, these buttons do not appear.

List or board

The List / Board switch, above the requests, changes how they are shown. The Board puts the requests in columns, one per status, in the order of their path; each column shows how many requests it holds. In the Business view, the Accepted and Estimated states share the “Accepted” column. A card opens the request’s details: that is where a change of status is decided, never by moving the card. Your choice is kept on your device.

How states match

The same story carries a state name in each view:
