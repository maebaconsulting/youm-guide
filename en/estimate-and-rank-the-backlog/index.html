Estimate and rank the backlog

The team estimates accepted stories, the Product Owner decides their order.

The backlog gathers the stories accepted by the Product Owner, estimated or not. It opens from the Technical view, Backlog view.

Rank the backlog

The Product Owner decides the order: the story at the top is the most important. They drag a story to its new place, or use the Move up and Move down arrows at the end of its row. The order is kept for everyone. A newly accepted story arrives at the bottom of the backlog.

If someone changed the backlog in the meantime, Youm refuses the new order and reloads the current backlog: just do it again.

Estimate a story

Only members of the team that runs the project and its Scrum Master estimate. A Product Owner who is not in the team cannot estimate.

  1. Open a story from the backlog.
  2. Choose Estimate, then a value from the project’s scale.
  3. Confirm with Estimate: the story becomes Estimated.

A story can be estimated again; the history keeps every estimate. The scale is set by the Scrum Master in the project’s practices:

  • Fibonacci: 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, and ”?” when the team does not know yet;
  • Integers: 0 to 999;
  • Custom: 2 to 15 numbers chosen by the Scrum Master, from smallest to largest, separated by semicolons;
  • No estimation: each story counts as 1, and the Estimate button does not appear.

When the Scrum Master changes the scale, estimates that no longer fit it are cleared: those stories become Accepted again, to be re-estimated, and the history records it. The Business view never shows estimates.

Return a story to proposals

The Product Owner can return an accepted or estimated story to proposals, with Return to proposals. It becomes Suggested again, leaves the backlog, and its estimate is cleared.

Import a specification

The Product Owner can create at once the requests of a specification kept in a spreadsheet. In the Backlog view, the More actions menu (“…”) offers Import: a window opens, where you choose a CSV file. Download the template gives a file ready to fill in.

Each row of the file becomes a request. Youm recognises these columns, in any order, in English or French:

  • Title, the only required one;
  • Why, which also takes a “Description” column;
  • Type: “New need” or “Defect”, a new need when the cell is empty;
  • Theme: a theme missing from the project is created;
  • Actor, if it is listed in the project settings;
  • Acceptance tests, one test per line in the cell.

Other columns are ignored. Save the file as “CSV UTF-8”: Youm reads semicolons as well as commas. An import holds at most 500 rows, 2,000 acceptance tests and 500 KB.

The preview creates nothing. It says how many requests will be created, which themes are new, and lists by number the rows that are corrected or left out: a title that is too long is shortened and its full version opens the “why”; a row without a title, or whose title already exists in the project or earlier in the file, is left out. Importing a corrected file again therefore does not create the same request twice.

Create the requests creates them all together, as Request sent: you then accept them like any request. If one of them cannot be created, none is. The history of each request shows the file it came from, and the audit log keeps a record of the import.
