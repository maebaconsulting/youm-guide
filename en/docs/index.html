Topics

Getting started

  • Sign in and sign upCreate your account, confirm your address, then sign in.
  • Create your organisationName the organisation that will bring your teams and projects together.
  • Invite peopleBring colleagues and outside people into the organisation.
  • Teams and projectsCreate the teams, then the projects they run, and share out the roles.
  • Languages, mobile and keyboardChoose your language, use Youm on a phone and navigate it entirely with the keyboard.
  • Business and Technical viewsTwo ways to see the same requests, depending on your role.

Requests and validation

  • Propose and follow a requestExpress a need or report a defect, then follow where the request stands.
  • Acceptance tests and validationCheck a story with its acceptance tests, move it to Awaiting validation, then validate it or request a correction.
  • Follow a request and be notifiedFollow a request, receive notifications in Youm and by email, and set your preferences.

Plan and build

  • Estimate and rank the backlogThe team estimates accepted stories, the Product Owner decides their order.
  • Features and themesGroup the stories that carry the same business value, called Themes on the business side.
  • Releases and sprintsSplit a Version into sprints, plan estimated stories into them and start the next sprint.
  • Tasks and Task BoardSplit stories into tasks and move them forward during the active sprint.
  • Sprint closing and velocityClose the sprint, read its summary and velocity, and find the sprint and release notes.

Collaborate

  • Working togetherSee other people’s changes live, and what happens when two people edit the same request.
  • Comment and attach filesDiscuss a request in Markdown, mention someone on the project and attach files.
  • Project wikiGather reference texts (regulations, decrees, procedures) and link them to the requests that cite them.
  • WhiteboardsDraw sketches and diagrams in the project, link them to the requests they illustrate, alone or together live.
  • Meeting recordsKeep the record of each meeting’s information, decisions and actions, and the register of the whole project.
  • Discussion and direct messagesTalk quickly in the project’s thread or one to one, mention someone and cite a request, an article, a whiteboard or a meeting record.

Administer

  • Plans and pricesSet the plans, their prices and their quotas from the back office.
  • Subscription and quotasChoose a plan, pay monthly or yearly and understand the limits.
  • Platform administrationLook up organizations and accounts, manage administrators, read the platform log.

Integrations

  • API and tokensConnect Youm to your tools with a personal token, and receive a project’s changes by webhook.
