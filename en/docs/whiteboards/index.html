Whiteboards

Draw sketches and diagrams in the project, link them to the requests they illustrate, alone or together live.

A whiteboard is for drawing what is hard to explain in writing: the screen you want, a flow, a diagram. It belongs to the project and can be linked to one or more requests, like a wiki article.

Turn whiteboards on

The option is off by default. The Scrum Master or the Product Owner opens Project settings, at the bottom of the side menu, then the Whiteboards tab. They tick the option and choose the Editing:

  • One at a time: you draw, then save. If someone saved in the meantime, Youm says so instead of overwriting their work, and offers to reload the board.
  • Live: everyone sees the others’ strokes and named cursors as they go. The board saves itself three seconds after the last stroke.

Switching from one mode to the other loses nothing, not even a stroke still waiting to be saved.

Create and draw

The Whiteboards entry of the side menu lists the project’s boards, with their thumbnail and the number of linked requests. New whiteboard asks for a title, then Create the whiteboard opens the full-screen editor.

In the editor, the toolbar offers shapes, arrows, text and free drawing. You can paste a screenshot or a photo, for example of a paper sketch. A board weighs at most 10 MB, images included.

In one-at-a-time mode, Save keeps the drawing. Closing the editor with unsaved strokes asks for confirmation.

Link to requests

From the editor, Linked requests links the board to a request of the project or removes it. From a request’s panel, the Whiteboards section shows the thumbnails of the linked boards, and Link a whiteboard adds one. The link shows in the request’s history.

Versions and going back

Versions lists the last 50 saves, with their author and date. Choosing a version shows it; Bring back this version takes it back as a new version: the current version stays in the list, nothing is lost. Live, a version is kept at most every five minutes.

Export, rename, delete

The board’s menu offers Export as PNG, Export as SVG, Rename and Delete the whiteboard. Only the board’s author, the Product Owner and the Scrum Master delete it, after confirmation. Whiteboards are also part of the project’s full export.

Who can do what

  • Anyone who sees the project sees its whiteboards.
  • The team, the Product Owner, the Scrum Master and business people draw.
  • Guests look without editing, like agents through the API.
  • On a phone, you view a board, zoom and move around, without editing it. On a tablet, you draw.
