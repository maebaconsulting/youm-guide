Discussion and direct messages

Talk quickly in the project’s thread or one to one, mention someone and cite a request, an article, a whiteboard or a meeting record.

Youm has two places for quick exchanges: the project discussion, a thread shared by everyone in the project, and direct messages, one to one between two people of the organisation. What concerns a specific request is commented on the request: the comment stays in its history (see the Comment and attach files chapter).

Turn on the project discussion

The option is off by default. The Product Owner or the Scrum Master opens Project settings, at the bottom of the side menu, then the Discussion tab, and ticks the option. The Discussion entry appears in the side menu, with the number of unread messages.

Write in the discussion

Write in Write a message, then Send. Text is formatted like a comment: bold, italics, lists, links. Messages arrive live for the others.

  • @ followed by a first name mentions someone in the project: they are notified in the bell, and by email if they have not read the discussion for five minutes.
  • A request reference, like PORTAIL-6, becomes a link to the request.
  • When you arrive in the discussion, New messages marks the first unread message.

Cite an item of the project

Type # followed by a few letters: Youm suggests the project’s requests, wiki articles, whiteboards and shared meeting records, at most five of each kind, ignoring accents and case. The arrows move through the list, Enter picks, Escape closes it.

A cited item becomes a link. On hover, or at the first touch on a phone, a preview gives its title and state, or a record’s date; a click opens it. Everyone sees citations according to their own rights: an item that was deleted, or that they are not allowed to see, shows as “unavailable item”, without a title.

Edit, delete, search

The menu of one of your messages offers Edit message, which adds the “edited” mention, and Delete message: its place stays, with Message deleted, so the conversation stays readable. The Product Owner and the Scrum Master can delete any message of the thread.

Search the discussion finds the messages containing a word. Older messages load as you scroll up. A person sends at most 20 messages per minute.

Direct messages

Direct messages are off by default. The owner or an administrator turns them on in the Organization settings, opened from the Members page, Direct messages section.

The messages icon, next to the bell, opens the conversations panel, with the number of unread messages. New message offers to Write to someone in the organisation. A conversation reads and writes like the project discussion: formatting, editing, deleting, citing with #. A cited request only becomes a link if both people can see it. Search your messages searches all your conversations.

A direct message notifies you like a mention: in the bell, and by email according to your preferences. The notification does not copy the message.

Privacy and departures

  • Nobody but the two people reads a direct message, administrators included, and direct messages are not part of exports.
  • The project discussion is part of the project’s full export.
  • Someone who leaves the project no longer sees its discussion; their earlier messages stay, signed with their name.
  • Someone who leaves the organisation keeps their past conversations read-only. The other person sees “is no longer in the organisation” and can no longer write to them.
  • A guest does not see the directory: they mention no one and start no conversation, but reply when written to.
  • Agents have no access to the discussion or to direct messages.
