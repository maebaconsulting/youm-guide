Meeting records

Keep the record of each meeting’s information, decisions and actions, and the register of the whole project.

A meeting record keeps track of a meeting: what was said, what was decided and what is left to do. Together, a project’s records form the Project meeting records, its register.

Turn meeting records on

The option is off by default. The Product Owner opens Project settings, at the bottom of the side menu, then the Meeting records tab, and ticks the option. The Meeting records entry then appears in the side menu, with My actions.

Write a record

The Product Owner and the Scrum Master write records. New meeting record asks for the meeting’s name and date, then Create the record opens the editor:

  • Participants: the people of the project who attended; Participants without an account: a contact at the client’s, for example, by name.
  • Information: what was said, in formatted text.
  • Decisions: one line per decision, with Add a decision.
  • Actions: one line per action, with Add an action. Each action has an Owner, who has an account, and a Due date, required.

A decision or an action can be linked to requests with Link a request: it then shows in the request’s history, and business people see why their request moved.

A record stays a draft, visible only to its writers, until Share. The whole project can then read it, each participant with an account receives it by email, and each action owner is notified in the bell. A shared record can still be corrected: each correction is kept under Corrections.

Follow the actions

An action has a status: To do, In progress, Blocked, Done or Dropped. Its owner or a writer changes it right in the actions table. Dropped asks for a short reason. An action neither done nor dropped whose due date has passed is flagged as overdue.

Turn into a request turns an action that needs development into a Suggested request, which the Product Owner accepts like any other.

My actions, in the side menu, gathers the open actions entrusted to you, the most urgent first.

Project meeting records

The Project meeting records tab brings all shared records together in one document: the information in meeting order, the decisions from newest to oldest, then the actions, open ones first. You filter it by period, by sprint and by owner.

Over time, one decision replaces another. Replaced by… links the old one to the new one: the old one stays in the register, struck through, with a pointer to the one that replaces it. Put back in force undoes the replacement.

The Document / Table switch shows the actions and decisions as tables, sortable by column. The Open and Overdue only filters narrow the list of actions.

Export

  • Export as PDF opens a page to print: choose Save as PDF in the print window. The project meeting records export the same way, with their filters.
  • Export to CSV produces the table of actions or decisions, readable by Excel.
  • Meeting records are part of the project’s full export.

Delete

A writer deletes a draft. A shared record can only be deleted by the Product Owner, after confirmation. The requests created from its actions stay.
