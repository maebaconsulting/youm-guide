Invite people

Bring colleagues and outside people into the organisation.

The owner and administrators of an organisation invite people to it by email. Members and guests do not see this feature.

Invite someone

  1. At the bottom of the side menu, choose Members. On a phone, the menu opens with the ☰ button, at the top left.
  2. Choose Invite someone.
  3. Enter the person’s email address and choose their Role, then Invite.

The person receives an email with a link valid for 7 days. The possible roles:

RoleWhat the person can do
AdministratorInvites people and manages members.
MemberWorks in the organisation’s projects.
GuestOutside person, such as a client: they will only see the projects shared with them.

Nobody becomes owner through an invitation.

Follow pending invitations

Below the form, each pending invitation shows its role and expiry date. An expired invitation is flagged in red: resend it.

  • Resend sends a new link, valid for 7 days again. The old link no longer works.
  • Revoke cancels the invitation, once confirmed: its link is refused.

Youm refuses a second invitation for an address that already has one pending: resend the first one instead.

Remove a person

The owner and administrators remove a person from the member list: Remove, then Confirm removal.

The person immediately loses access to the organisation and their roles in teams and projects, and their tasks are left without an owner. What they wrote stays in place and their account is untouched: a new invitation brings them back. Invitations they sent that are still pending are revoked.

The owner cannot be removed: transfer the organisation first. If the person is the only Product Owner of a project, appoint another one first.

Accept an invitation

When opening the link, the invited person signs in, or creates their account, with the address that received the invitation. If they create their account, the link in the confirmation email brings them back to their invitation, whichever browser they open it in.

Once signed in, they see the proposed organisation and role, choose Accept the invitation and land in the organisation. An expired, revoked or already used link is refused with the reason.

If the invitation was opened with the wrong account, Sign out, below the card, lets them sign in with the right one: the invitation is kept.

Belong to several organisations

The same person can belong to several organisations. Their names then appear in a menu at the top left, which switches from one to another without signing in again. Each organisation’s data stays separate.

Read the audit log

The owner and the administrators choose Organization settings on the Members page, then open Audit log. Each line gives the date, the action, what it concerns and its author: invitations, roles, rights to validate, validated or deleted stories, waivers, closings and exports. An action taken through the API also carries the name of the token used. Everyday work does not appear in it.

Export the log downloads the whole log as CSV.

Open access for support

To get help with a problem, the owner or an administrator opens the Support access section of the Organization settings: Open for 1 hour, Open for 24 hours or Open for 7 days. During that time, Youm support sees your projects and requests read-only, without being able to change anything. Withdraw access closes it at once. Opening, each look and withdrawal go to the audit log.

Hand over or delete the organisation

In the Organization settings, the Danger zone is folded. Only the owner can:

  • Hand over the organisation to a member or an administrator: that person becomes owner, and you stay administrator;
  • Delete the organisation: type its name in the confirmation window. Its projects, stories and files disappear for everyone, with no way back, and its subscription is cancelled at once. Export first what you want to keep.
