Changelog

Changelog

Version 0.12.0

  • Request discussion: Markdown comments and mentions of people on the project.
  • Attached files, up to 10 MB, stored separately for each organisation and downloaded through a one-minute link.
  • A stakeholder comments on their requests and attaches files to them.

Version 0.11.0

  • Real time: open views update in less than two seconds, with no leak between organisations.
  • Simultaneous edits: an edit started before someone else’s is refused, with the Reload the request button.
  • Flaps: a changing status label flips letter by letter, unless motion is reduced.

Version 0.10.0

  • Sprint closing: stories awaiting validation are carried over in their state, the others go back to Planned, or to the backlog after the release’s last sprint.
  • Velocity counted on validation, frozen closing summary and average velocity.
  • Sprint and release notes: validated stories and waivers with their reasons.
  • The Product Owner cancels a validation during the sprint in which it happened.

Version 0.9.0

  • Ordered acceptance tests, Passed or Failed results recorded while the story is In progress or Awaiting validation.
  • Waivers granted by the Product Owner only, test by test, with a reason kept in the history.
  • Move to Awaiting validation by a human on the team, refused while a task is open.
  • Validate or request a correction in place, from the business board, with the project or release staging link.

Version 0.8.0

  • Story, urgent and recurring tasks, with remaining time in hours and a PORTAIL-Tn identifier.
  • Sprint Task Board: To do, In progress and Done columns, one row per story and lanes for urgent and recurring tasks.
  • Initial estimate frozen on first start, blockers flagged with a reason, limit of urgent tasks in progress.
  • A story whose tasks are all done is flagged “Ready for Awaiting validation”, without changing state.

Version 0.7.0

  • Releases: creation with a vision, sprints generated at the project’s length, a single active release.
  • Estimated stories planned into sprints, prerequisites respected, planned capacity.
  • Activation of the next sprint, one at a time; a reason is required to change an active sprint.
  • Business view: product goal, Version targeted by each request, its date and its vision.

Version 0.6.0

  • Features: creation, colour, business value, ranking, calculated status (To do, In progress, Done) and closing.
  • A story can be attached to a Feature, whose colour it takes.
  • Business view: optional grouping by theme.
  • Technical stories created by the team, absent from the Business view.

Version 0.5.0

  • Stories estimated by the team and the Scrum Master on the project’s scale: Fibonacci, integers, custom or no estimation.
  • The Product Owner ranks the backlog by drag and drop or with Move up and Move down; the order is kept.
  • A story can be returned to proposals, its estimate cleared.

Version 0.4.0

  • Propose a request (“New need” or “Defect”) with a readable identifier; edit or delete it until it is accepted.
  • “My requests” board: business status, who the request is with and the next step, “Mine” and “All” tabs.
  • The Product Owner accepts, declines with a reason or reopens a proposal from the Technical view.
  • Greyed-out actions with their reason and rule code; history of each request.

Version 0.3.0

  • Teams: creation, members and Scrum Master.
  • Projects: key, product goal and team; Product Owners and stakeholders; right to validate granted to a stakeholder.
  • Project practices set by the Scrum Master: estimation scale, sprint length, automatic assignment, urgent task limit.
  • A guest only sees the projects shared with them.

Version 0.2.0

  • Email invitations with a role (administrator, member or guest) and a link valid for 7 days; resend and revoke.
  • Membership of several organisations and switching organisation without signing in again.

Version 0.1.0

  • Account creation, email address confirmation and sign-in.
  • Organisation creation with its identifier.
  • Business and Technical views, still empty.
  • Interface in French and English.

This guide describes Youm 0.12.0, the version of November 2, 2026 (1c3c9d7). Changelog
