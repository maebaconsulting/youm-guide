Changelog

Changelog

Version 0.63.0

  • Less stacking, finishing touches: in a story’s details, the actions that move it forward stay at the foot of the panel whatever its length, and the panel widens on a large screen; deleting a story is confirmed in a window, and adding a test opens through New test once the story has some. A sprint chosen on the timeline opens in a panel without leaving the timeline, and its notes no longer repeat its stories. On a phone, the business home shows “In progress” and “Coming up” side by side, the Task Board hides empty states and notifications rise from the bottom of the screen. Subscription plans sit side by side; the plans back office is a table, each plan edited in its panel. The Product Owner finds Import again in the “…” menu of the business view. When grouped by theme, the theme dot is no longer repeated on each line.

Version 0.62.0

  • Less stacking, continued: a project page reads by tabs (roles, writing, practices, agents, webhooks), with a summary for whoever does not set a section; adding a person or a webhook opens in a window, and a webhook is managed in its panel. My account shows the address and the password as lines, changed in a window, like a new token; deleting the account moves to the danger zone. New Feature and new release open in a window, a Feature is edited in its panel, and its rare actions are in its “…” menu. Plan a story opens a bubble under the sprint, staging links move to settings, and closing a sprint is confirmed in a window. Importing a specification and deleting a view are in the view’s “…” menu. The button of a destructive confirmation is red.

Version 0.61.0

  • Less stacking: creating a project or a team, importing from iceScrum, inviting someone and proposing a request open in a window; a team is managed in a side panel. On the Members page, the list comes first; API access, the assistant, the audit log and the danger zone move to the Organization settings. Deleting a view, a task, a file, a comment, a test or a session, revoking an invitation and removing a member now ask for confirmation. On a phone, the top bar fits on one line, the rest in the avatar’s menu.

Version 0.60.0

  • Technical view board at full width: each column gives its number of stories and its points, keeps its header visible and scrolls on its own; denser cards with Feature, owners, progress of tasks and tests. A click on a Feature or initials filters the view, lanes by Feature as an option, quick add of a story in the Suggested column. On the Task Board, tasks also move by drag and drop.

Version 0.59.0

  • Technical view at full width, side menu that folds into a column of icons (the choice is kept on your device), and a new By Feature layout: each Feature, foldable, with its number of stories, its points and a progress bar (one mark per story, validated out of total, percentage), then its stories with their status, the owners of their tasks, their points and their tasks done.

Version 0.58.0

  • Writing assistant: turned off by default, an administrator turns it on with the organization’s Anthropic key, encrypted and never shown again. In a story’s details, technical view, the team gets a rewording in the project’s format, Given / When / Then tests and INVEST remarks; nothing is saved without “Use this suggestion”.

Version 0.57.0

  • Agent sessions: an agent, or a team member, records each work session on a story (duration, cost, iterations, model, link to the trace), through the API or the record_session MCP tool. Sessions and their total appear in the technical details, next to the points, for the team only.

Version 0.56.0

  • MCP server for agents: at the API’s /v1/mcp address, an agent lists the stories ready to start, takes one, attaches its evidence (a sentence and a link) and marks it ready for review. Evidence appears in the technical details, for the team only.

Version 0.55.0

  • Technical details of a story: in a project open to agents, a collapsed section for the team only spells out the desired behaviour, key interfaces, testable criteria, out of scope, implementation by an agent or a human, size for an agent and review effort. An agent can prefill it through the API. Nothing changes for business users.

Version 0.54.0

  • Agent work ready for review: an agent marks the In progress story it has finished “Ready for review”; the team member who reviews it and moves it to Awaiting validation signs it “Reviewed by”. Nothing changes for business users.

Version 0.53.0

  • “Written by AI” mention: a story, comment, task or history line written by an agent carries this mention next to its name, shortened to “AI” in lists and on the Task Board.

Version 0.52.0

  • Specification import: the Product Owner creates at once the requests of a CSV file, with their themes, actors and acceptance tests, after a preview that lists the rows corrected or left out; a file template can be downloaded from the import screen.

Version 0.51.0

  • Version report: a validated request shows what was accepted at the time of validation, and a line flags an actor or theme changed since; the CSV gains a “Changed since validation” column.

Version 0.50.0

  • Version report: for one version, the requests, their tests, their waivers and every validation decision, to print or download as CSV, for the Product Owner, delegated validators and administrators.

Version 0.49.0

  • Agent development: a project option, off by default and set by the Scrum Master, opens the project to the organisation’s agents; without it, an agent does not act in the project and receives no task there.

Version 0.48.0

  • Agents: an administrator declares non-human members, who work through a service token in a team, without validating or deciding, and do not count in the billed seats.

Version 0.47.0

  • Webhooks: a project sends its chosen changes to an HTTPS address, signed with HMAC SHA-256, with retries and turning off after ten failures in a row.
  • Audit log: an action taken through the API carries the name of the token used, on screen and in the export.
  • Public API: the limit of 300 requests per minute applies to the token, whichever server answers.

Version 0.46.0

  • Public API: an administrator turns on API access, each member creates personal read or write tokens limited to one organisation, and the OpenAPI description is published.

Version 0.45.0

  • Members: the owner and administrators remove a person from the organisation, and billed seats follow.
  • Payment: the billing address is required, a VAT number can be entered, and VAT is added to the pre-tax price when it is enabled.

Version 0.44.0

  • Validate: the test summary appears before confirming, and the home page confirms the decision.

Version 0.43.1

  • More even text on Linux, with better letter spacing.
  • Board: white cards, the state reads from the column.

Version 0.43.0

  • iceScrum import: an XML or ZIP export becomes a Youm project, after checking people, states and gaps. Numbers, states, tests, tasks and comments are kept.

Version 0.42.1

  • My account: the badge opens the page without leaving a bubble on top.

Version 0.42.0

  • My account: change your email address, confirmed from both the old and the new mailbox.

Version 0.41.0

  • Organisation: the owner hands it over to a member, or deletes it with its projects and files. The subscription is then cancelled at once.

Version 0.40.0

  • My account: change your password, download your data and delete your account. The work stays in the organisation, without the person’s name.

Version 0.39.0

  • Audit log: the Members page traces the organisation’s critical actions, on access, validation and exports. The log exports to CSV.

Version 0.38.0

  • Export: the owner and the administrators download all the organisation’s data, and the Product Owner that of their project, in an archive with the attached files and the history.

Version 0.37.0

  • Saved backlogs: the Product Owner and the Scrum Master save a view filtered by state, type or Feature, shared with the whole team in the menu. Each list view shows its breakdown.

Version 0.36.0

  • Business view: the home screen shows where the current version stands, with its due date and the number of validated requests.

Version 0.35.0

  • Reports: what remains and what is done in the release, sprint by sprint and by story type, against its scope. A finished release keeps what remained, even after its stories return to the backlog.

Version 0.34.0

  • Reports: a new view shows the remaining work of the sprint, in hours or points, and velocity against planned capacity. Each sprint keeps its snapshots from activation, closing and every day of activity.

Version 0.33.0

  • Access: forgotten password, with an email link to choose a new one. The failure message no longer mentions signing in during a sign-up.

Version 0.32.1

  • Plans: on a phone, the back office button shows its name and prices come first in each plan, with the texts folded. The top bar no longer overflows.

Version 0.32.0

  • Subscription: the owner subscribes to a plan and pays through Stripe, monthly or yearly. Plan quotas apply once payment is enabled.

Version 0.31.0

  • Plans: the home page presents the Free, Developer and Teams plans, monthly or yearly, and a back office sets their prices and quotas.
  • Presentation page: the validation diagram shows that a failed test blocks everything until it passes.

Version 0.30.1

  • Home: a visitor without a session reaches the presentation page from the application address, and the site root no longer shows a waiting page.

Version 0.30.0

  • Board: cards carry a stronger wash of their state colour, over their full height.
  • Presentation page: new backgrounds. A field seen from above, whose plots light up in turn, and bands of blue on true black.

Version 0.29.1

  • Access screens: the logo and the Back to home link return to the presentation page.

Version 0.29.0

  • Board: each card carries its type on a tab, a tint of its state colour and, for the team, a bar with one segment per acceptance test.

Version 0.28.1

  • Presentation page redone: dark ground, the application shown large, Business and Technical view tabs, Propose, Plan and Validate sections, animated diagrams of a story’s cycle, the sprint and validation.

Version 0.28.0

  • New Youm presentation page, in French and English: one story read from the business side and the team side, with real screenshots, and sign-up in one click.

Version 0.27.1

  • New Youm logo in the application and the guide, with its tab icon.

Version 0.27.0

  • Tighter board: columns as bins with a solid state label and a count, empty columns folded, and dense cards with identifier, initials, title, Feature and labels.
  • Task Board: solid labels and counts at the top of columns, dense cards with the owner’s initials next to the identifier.

Version 0.26.0

  • Feature colours: a new Feature automatically gets the project’s least used colour, and the picker shows free colours, then those already taken with their Feature’s name, reusable in one click.

Version 0.25.2

  • Fewer instructions on screen: needless help text is gone, and useful details move into a bubble, by clicking the “i” next to the field.

Version 0.25.1

  • Sign out from the organisation creation and invitation screens, which also show the signed-in account.

Version 0.25.0

  • View selector in Planning, Timeline or List: the Timeline places Versions and their sprints at their dates, with one colour per Version, each Feature’s share and a today marker.
  • Version and sprint states in colour.

Version 0.24.1

  • Finishing touches: states readable in full, lighter dropdowns, better balanced columns, and on the home screen the display tools next to the tabs.

Version 0.24.0

  • Lighter request details: Prerequisites and Feature are lines of text you can change with a click, and the actor is chosen in the “As a” sentence.
  • The version and its vision are revealed on click, like the reason for a greyed-out action. No more instructions under the buttons, and no more rule codes on screen.
  • In the Technical view, only possible actions are shown.

Version 0.23.0

  • New Business view home: a sentence says what awaits you, then blocks show the decisions to make, what is in progress and what is coming up. The tabs, list and board stay below.

Version 0.22.0

  • People now appear as initials, in a colour that follows them everywhere: in a request’s details, its history, the discussion and the Task Board. Selecting them reveals their address.

Version 0.21.0

  • New typeface, Poppins, and tighter corners on every screen.
  • Bolder, better separated Feature colours.
  • Lighter dropdowns: a soft background and an arrow, no outline.

Version 0.20.0

  • Optional question “How will you know it worked?” when proposing a request, for projects in the User story format: each line becomes a test for the team to rephrase.
  • Given / When / Then template when entering an acceptance test, and rephrasing a test from a story’s details.

Version 0.19.0

  • Story format chosen by the Product Owner: Simple or User story (“As a…, I want…, so that…”), with the project’s actors; an outline offered for a defect. The Business view stays simple.

Version 0.18.0

  • Formatting: a bar (bold, italic, lists, checklist, link, code) and a preview for a request’s “Why”, a Feature’s description and comments.
  • Team notes on each story, in the Technical view only.

Version 0.17.0

  • Features in colour: the Feature’s name, in its tint, goes with each story in the lists and the board, whose cards carry a band of that colour; twelve tints to choose from instead of eight.

Version 0.16.0

  • Colours: each request status has its tint, in its pill, its icon and the Task Board columns; so does a story’s type.
  • Board display: requests in columns by status, as an alternative to the list.
  • Lighter screens: no more introduction sentences or inactive buttons (Filter, Sort, search); the “With” column is gone, the next step already says it.

Version 0.15.0

  • Technical view: a side menu replaces the tabs, with the projects then the project’s views and their counts; on a phone, a drawer opened by “Menu”.

Version 0.14.0

  • The chosen language is saved on the account: it follows the person across devices and in their emails.
  • Keyboard: “Skip to content” link, focus kept inside a request’s or task’s details, then returned when it closes.
  • Contrast: the grey of secondary text is darker to reach 4.5 to 1 on every background (WCAG 2.2 AA).

Version 0.13.0

  • Following a request: its author and commenters follow it automatically; anyone can follow or unfollow it.
  • Live notifications in the bell for status changes, comments, mentions and requests awaiting validation.
  • Notification emails according to each person’s preference: all, mentions only or none.

Version 0.12.0

  • Request discussion: Markdown comments and mentions of people on the project.
  • Attached files, up to 10 MB, stored separately for each organisation and downloaded through a one-minute link.
  • A stakeholder comments on their requests and attaches files to them.

Version 0.11.0

  • Real time: open views update in less than two seconds, with no leak between organisations.
  • Simultaneous edits: an edit started before someone else’s is refused, with the Reload the request button.
  • Flaps: a changing status label flips letter by letter, unless motion is reduced.

Version 0.10.0

  • Sprint closing: stories awaiting validation are carried over in their state, the others go back to Planned, or to the backlog after the release’s last sprint.
  • Velocity counted on validation, frozen closing summary and average velocity.
  • Sprint and release notes: validated stories and waivers with their reasons.
  • The Product Owner cancels a validation during the sprint in which it happened.

Version 0.9.0

  • Ordered acceptance tests, Passed or Failed results recorded while the story is In progress or Awaiting validation.
  • Waivers granted by the Product Owner only, test by test, with a reason kept in the history.
  • Move to Awaiting validation by a human on the team, refused while a task is open.
  • Validate or request a correction in place, from the business board, with the project or release staging link.

Version 0.8.0

  • Story, urgent and recurring tasks, with remaining time in hours and a PORTAIL-Tn identifier.
  • Sprint Task Board: To do, In progress and Done columns, one row per story and lanes for urgent and recurring tasks.
  • Initial estimate frozen on first start, blockers flagged with a reason, limit of urgent tasks in progress.
  • A story whose tasks are all done is flagged “Ready for Awaiting validation”, without changing state.

Version 0.7.0

  • Releases: creation with a vision, sprints generated at the project’s length, a single active release.
  • Estimated stories planned into sprints, prerequisites respected, planned capacity.
  • Activation of the next sprint, one at a time; a reason is required to change an active sprint.
  • Business view: product goal, Version targeted by each request, its date and its vision.

Version 0.6.0

  • Features: creation, colour, business value, ranking, calculated status (To do, In progress, Done) and closing.
  • A story can be attached to a Feature, whose colour it takes.
  • Business view: optional grouping by theme.
  • Technical stories created by the team, absent from the Business view.

Version 0.5.0

  • Stories estimated by the team and the Scrum Master on the project’s scale: Fibonacci, integers, custom or no estimation.
  • The Product Owner ranks the backlog by drag and drop or with Move up and Move down; the order is kept.
  • A story can be returned to proposals, its estimate cleared.

Version 0.4.0

  • Propose a request (“New need” or “Defect”) with a readable identifier; edit or delete it until it is accepted.
  • “My requests” board: business status, who the request is with and the next step, “Mine” and “All” tabs.
  • The Product Owner accepts, declines with a reason or reopens a proposal from the Technical view.
  • Greyed-out actions with their reason and rule code; history of each request.

Version 0.3.0

  • Teams: creation, members and Scrum Master.
  • Projects: key, product goal and team; Product Owners and stakeholders; right to validate granted to a stakeholder.
  • Project practices set by the Scrum Master: estimation scale, sprint length, automatic assignment, urgent task limit.
  • A guest only sees the projects shared with them.

Version 0.2.0

  • Email invitations with a role (administrator, member or guest) and a link valid for 7 days; resend and revoke.
  • Membership of several organisations and switching organisation without signing in again.

Version 0.1.0

  • Account creation, email address confirmation and sign-in.
  • Organisation creation with its identifier.
  • Business and Technical views, still empty.
  • Interface in French and English.
