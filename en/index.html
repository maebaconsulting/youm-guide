User guide

Youm’s screens, step by step. Every screenshot comes from the version described below, in English, on desktop and on phone.

Chapters

  1. Sign in and sign up

    Create your account, confirm your address, then sign in.

  2. Create your organisation

    Name the organisation that will bring your teams and projects together.

  3. Invite people

    Bring colleagues and outside people into the organisation.

  4. Teams and projects

    Create the teams, then the projects they run, and share out the roles.

  5. Propose and follow a request

    Express a need or report a defect, then follow where the request stands.

  6. Estimate and rank the backlog

    The team estimates accepted stories, the Product Owner decides their order.

  7. Features and themes

    Group the stories that carry the same business value, called Themes on the business side.

  8. Releases and sprints

    Split a Version into sprints, plan estimated stories into them and start the next sprint.

  9. Tasks and Task Board

    Split stories into tasks and move them forward during the active sprint.

  10. Acceptance tests and validation

    Check a story with its acceptance tests, move it to Awaiting validation, then validate it or request a correction.

  11. Sprint closing and velocity

    Close the sprint, read its summary and velocity, and find the sprint and release notes.

  12. Working together

    See other people’s changes live, and what happens when two people edit the same request.

  13. Comment and attach files

    Discuss a request in Markdown, mention someone on the project and attach files.

  14. Follow a request and be notified

    Follow a request, receive notifications in Youm and by email, and set your preferences.

  15. Languages, mobile and keyboard

    Choose your language, use Youm on a phone and navigate it entirely with the keyboard.

  16. Business and Technical views

    Two ways to see the same requests, depending on your role.

  17. Plans and prices

    Set the plans, their prices and their quotas from the back office.

  18. Subscription and quotas

    Choose a plan, pay monthly or yearly and understand the limits.

  19. API and tokens

    Connect Youm to your tools with a personal token, and receive a project’s changes by webhook.

  20. Project wiki

    Gather reference texts (regulations, decrees, procedures) and link them to the requests that cite them.

  21. Platform administration

    Look up organizations and accounts, manage administrators, read the platform log.

This guide describes Youm 0.80.0, the version of November 12, 2026 (af49fe6). Changelog
