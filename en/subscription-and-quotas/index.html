Subscription and quotas

Choose a plan, pay monthly or yearly and understand the limits.

Every organisation starts on the Free plan. Its owner can subscribe to a paid plan, monthly or yearly. Nobody else sees this screen.

Choose a plan

The Subscription button in the top bar shows the current plan and the organisation’s usage. Under Change plan, pick the billing period, then Choose this plan. Payment happens on the secure page of our provider, Stripe. The billing address is required there, along with the VAT number for a company: both appear on invoices.

The subscription appears as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Manage the subscription

Once subscribed, Manage subscription and invoices opens the Stripe area. There you change card and billing address, download invoices and cancel. To change plan, cancel, then choose the new plan when the paid period ends.

When a cancelled subscription ends, the organisation returns to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted.

Quotas

A plan can limit the number of projects and active members. A reached quota refuses the next addition and never removes anything.

Guests do not count. A pending invitation already counts as an active member.

When a plan is billed per active member, the number of seats follows each arrival and departure, pro rata for the current period. A departure is deducted from the next invoice, with no immediate refund.
