Propose and follow a request

Express a need or report a defect, then follow where the request stands.

Anyone who can see a project can propose a request in it, a guest stakeholder included. No knowledge of Scrum is needed.

Propose a request

  1. On My requests, choose Propose a need.
  2. Under What, say in a few words what you want to get (100 characters at most, without reusing the title of another request in the project).
  3. Under Why, describe the problem it solves, and for whom. The formatting bar adds bold, a list or a checklist; the Preview tab shows the result.
  4. Choose the Request type: New need, or Defect if something does not work as expected.
  5. If the project uses the User story format, you can answer How will you know it worked?, one idea per line. The team turns them into acceptance tests.
  6. Choose Send the request.

The request gets a readable identifier, made of the project key and a number, for example PORTAIL-4. It is then “Request sent” and waits for the Product Owner.

Follow your requests

The Business view home starts with a sentence saying what awaits you. Then come the Awaiting validation block, if there is a decision to make, In progress and Coming up. Choose a request in a block to open its details.

Further down, My requests opens on the Mine tab: your own requests. The All tab shows those of the whole project. For each request, the board shows:

  • its Status, in business words;
  • the Next step.

If there are several projects, choose one in the Project list at the top right of the board.

Edit, delete, understand a decline

Choose a request to open its details. Until it is accepted, its author can Edit or Delete it.

When the Product Owner declines a request, they always give the reason, shown in red on the request. A declined request is kept: its author can edit it, but no longer delete it, and only the Product Owner can reopen it to put it back on hold.

A greyed-out button is an action you cannot take: select it to see why.

The History keeps every change: the field, the old and new value, the person and the date.
