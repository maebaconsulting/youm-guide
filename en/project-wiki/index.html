Project wiki

Gather reference texts (regulations, decrees, procedures) and link them to the requests that cite them.

Each project has its wiki: reference articles that requests cite, such as a decree, a retention rule or an internal procedure. An article is not a requirement: it has no state and no validation, and never blocks a request.

Read and search

The wiki opens from the Wiki entry of the side menu in the Technical view, and from the Wiki tab in the Business view. The search box looks through article titles and text, ignoring accents and case. Each article shows how many requests cite it and when it was last changed.

Write an article

New article opens a window: a title, unique in the project wiki, a Markdown text and, if you wish, the link to the official source. You can then attach files, for instance the decree as a PDF (10 MB at most per file).

The team, the Scrum Master, the Product Owner and stakeholders write and edit articles. Guests of the organization only read them. Only an article’s author and the Product Owner delete it, after confirming.

An article opens in a panel: its text, its source, its files, the requests that cite it and its history (who changed what, and when).

Link an article to a request

An article links to several requests of the same project, and a request can cite several articles. You link from the article (Link a request) or from the request, in its References section (Link an article). The request’s history keeps every reference added or removed; if the article is deleted, it records “Reference removed”.

The References section also shows in the Business view: clicking a reference opens the article.

Articles, their links and their files are part of the project export and of the organization export. Through the API, the same actions go through the createArticle, editArticle, deleteArticle, linkArticle and unlinkArticle commands; an article written by an agent carries the “Written by AI” mention.
