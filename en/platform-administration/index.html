Platform administration

Look up organizations and accounts, manage administrators, read the platform log.

This chapter is for platform administrators, the people who run Youm. Organizations do not see this console. It looks things up: it never changes a customer’s data, and only shows the content of their projects when the organization has opened support access.

Open the console

The Administration button in the top bar opens the console. It only opens with two-factor authentication: the first time, the console offers to turn it on (see Manage your account in the Sign in and sign up chapter).

Organizations

The Organizations tab lists every organization, newest first: owner, plan, subscription status, billed members, agents, projects, space taken by files, last change to a request and creation date. A search covers the name, the identifier and the owner; a list filters by plan.

Clicking an organization opens its record in a panel: its members and their roles, its projects with their key and number of requests, and a link to the customer in Stripe. Opening a record is written to the platform log and to the organization’s audit log, which its owner can read.

Users

The Users tab lists accounts: address, sign-up, last sign-in, organizations and roles, two-factor authentication on or off, account blocked or active. A search covers the address. Agents are not listed.

The … menu of a row blocks or unblocks an account, and resets its two-factor authentication. A blocked account can no longer sign in: its sessions and API tokens are cut off, and what it wrote stays in place. A platform administrator cannot be blocked while they hold that role. Resetting a person’s two-factor authentication, when they have lost their phone, asks for a new code; you cannot reset your own. Either way, an email tells the person.

Support access

When an organization has opened support access, its record says so, with the end time, and offers Open read-only. Its projects and requests then show under a status banner that recalls the organization and the end time. Nothing can be changed there, and each look goes to the platform log and to the organization’s log. Without access opened by the organization, this button does not appear.

Administrators

The Administrators tab lists platform administrators. Add an administrator asks for the address of an existing Youm account; Remove takes the role away, except from the last administrator. Both actions ask for a new 6-digit code, even when your session has already passed two-factor authentication.

Log

The Log tab keeps every administrator action: record viewed, administrator added or removed, plan saved, log exported. Export as CSV downloads the full log.
